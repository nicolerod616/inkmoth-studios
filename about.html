About InkMoth

Art that is a little strange on purpose.

I created InkMoth Studios as a place for all of the different kinds of art and design work I enjoy making. My work ranges from custom paintings and signs to logos, illustrations, clothing designs, and prints.

I tend to lean toward darker styles, bold line work, and black and white designs with small amounts of color. At the same time, I like being able to change my style depending on the project and what someone is looking for.

A very dramatic photo of me pretending I know what I'm doing.

My Style

Dark, detailed, and a little weird.

I enjoy detailed line work, gothic and grunge-inspired designs, darker imagery, and adding small unexpected details to my work.

Custom Work

Not everything has to look the same.

I also create brighter and more playful custom pieces like birthday signs, window art, and other designs based on what the client actually wants.

Why InkMoth?

A little bit of everything.

InkMoth gives me a place to combine illustration, painting, design, and whatever other creative idea I decide to start next.
